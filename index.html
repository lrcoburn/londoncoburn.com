About

We build clean, fast, and affordable websites for individuals, small groups, and organizations. We specialize in student-focused sites that highlight achievements, portfolios, and institutional excellence.

Coburn Studio is a small web design firm founded by students who understand the unique needs of educational communities. We design accessible, mobile‑first websites that are easy to maintain and look professional. Every project reflects meticulous attention to detail, thoughtful information architecture, and a commitment to performance.

Our approach combines practical web development with a fine-art sensibility. Rather than templated solutions, we craft bespoke digital experiences—whether a photography portfolio, institutional site, or student showcase—that communicate clearly and feel intentional. We bring a discipline-driven, deadline-conscious mindset to every engagement.

What we offer:

  • Custom responsive web design (HTML, CSS, modern standards)
  • Portfolio & gallery sites for photographers, artists, and creatives
  • Institutional pages for schools, clubs, and organizations
  • Domain registration and hosting guidance
  • SEO fundamentals and performance optimization
  • Ongoing maintenance and content updates

Typical student packages include a fully responsive design, professional branding consultation, domain & hosting setup, and deployment guidance. We work flexibly with organizations and institutions looking for a scalable, maintainable web presence.

Whether you're a student, small organization, or creative looking for an affordable, polished digital home, we'd be happy to discuss your project. We offer free initial consultations and transparent pricing.
